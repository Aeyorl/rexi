# Bool Protocol — Executive Summary

### What is Bool?
**Bool** is a token launchpad built natively on **Robinhood Chain**. It allows creators, communities, and projects to launch tokens where **holders passively earn real stock and crypto dividends** (such as Apple stock, Tesla stock, ETH, or USD) simply by holding the token in their wallet—no staking, locking, or complicated DeFi steps required.

---

### How It Works (The Core Mechanism)
1. **Launch**: A creator deploys a new token (e.g., `$OWEGO`) and chooses its reward asset (e.g., Apple stock `$AAPL`, Wrapped Ether `$WETH`, or `$USDG`).
2. **Distribute**: The project team or community deposits reward assets into the launchpad dividend pool.
3. **Earn**: Every token holder automatically accrues their proportional share of the dividends in real-time. Holders can claim their accumulated stock or crypto dividends at any time directly into their wallet.

---

### Reward Assets Supported
Through Robinhood Chain’s tokenized asset ecosystem, Bool natively supports:
* 🍎 **Apple Stock ($AAPL)** — Tokenized Robinhood stock
* ⚡ **Tesla Stock ($TSLA)** — Tokenized Robinhood stock
* 💎 **Ethereum ($WETH)** — Wrapped Ether
* 💵 **Global Dollar ($USDG)** — Paxos / Robinhood institutional stablecoin

---

### Revenue Model & Fee Distribution
Whenever rewards are distributed to a token, the protocol automatically routes the capital:
* **67.5%** ➔ **Token Holders** (Direct dividend payout)
* **10.0%** ➔ **Desks Treasury** (Institutional partners & trading desks)
* **10.0%** ➔ **Buyback Treasury** (Protocol token buybacks & liquidity)
* **5.0%** ➔ **Protocol Treasury** (Protocol development & reserves)
* **7.5%** ➔ **Platform Operations** (Infrastructure & retained buffer)
